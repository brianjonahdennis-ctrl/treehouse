# Sign up for Treasure Hunts near you

Every Treasure Hunt now belongs to one state. Anyone can see it, but only members in that state can register.
After you register you see how many others registered and a countdown to the start.
When the hunt goes live, the countdown turns into a "Start treasure hunt" button.
Prizes can now show pictures and videos.

On Home, My Treehouse and Of the Week are now one box with two tabs, and treehouse Business is coming soon.
