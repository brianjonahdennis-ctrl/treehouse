# Follow the clues and find the treasure

- Find Treasure Hunt in Games, follow clues through public places and check in at each stop.
- Your phone handles the camera and compass. From AR, open Google Maps to follow its walking route, turn prompts and blue line.
- Admins can set several start and treasure locations, rename checkpoints and choose coin and treasure designs.
- Join to get the nearest route; see only your own standing until it ends, and share location only when choosing your route or checking in.
- Treehouse Coins are game points, not cash. Stay aware of traffic and keep to public places.
