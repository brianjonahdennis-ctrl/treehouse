# Follow the clues and find the treasure

- A new Treasure Hunt is coming to Games, with real public places and checkpoint clues.
- Join a hunt, follow the route and check in when you reach each stop.
- Your phone handles the camera, compass and nearby direction. From the AR view, open Google Maps for its walking route, turn prompts and blue line.
- Your location is checked only when you claim a checkpoint.
- Collect Treehouse Coins as in-game points; they are not cash.
- Keep aware of traffic and surroundings, and never enter private or closed places.
