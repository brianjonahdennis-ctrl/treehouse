# Release note: My Treehouse and Treehouse chats (15 Nov 2026)

## In-app announcement (Product Announcements)

**Title** (100 max)
🌳 Introducing My Treehouse: grow your own network

**Preview** (160 max)
See everyone who joined Treehouse through you, watch your tree grow, and send message requests to people in your Treehouse.

**Category:** New feature
**Button:** Open My Treehouse → `/home/treehouse`

**Body**

Your Treehouse just got its own home. 🌳

**My Treehouse** shows everyone who joined Treehouse through your invite link, and everyone *they* invited after them.

**What you can do:**
- **Share your link your way:** copy it, send it to WhatsApp, Instagram, Telegram and more, or show your personal **QR code** so friends can scan it.
- **See your numbers:** your direct referrals (people you invited), your network (people they invited) and your total.
- **Explore Your Tree:** tap anyone to see when they joined and how big their own network is. Switch to List view to search, filter by level and sort.
- **Start a conversation:** send a **message request** to anyone in your Treehouse. They can accept or decline, just like any other request.
- **Treehouse chats in your Inbox:** chats with people in your Treehouse now have their own **Treehouse** column in your Inbox and a Treehouse filter in Chats.

**Good to know:**
- Everyone stays anonymous. Your tree shows nicknames only, never usernames, phone numbers or photos.
- Being in someone's tree doesn't make you friends or open a chat on its own. A chat starts only when they accept your request.
- A referral counts when your friend creates their Treehouse profile soon after signing up with your link. Link clicks alone don't count.
- Referral rewards are coming soon. Keep growing your tree!

Find it on your **Home** screen (My Treehouse card) or in **Profile**.
