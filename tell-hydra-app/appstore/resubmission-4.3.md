# App Store resubmission after the 4.3(b) rejection (19 Nov 2026)

Apple rejected version 1.4 (build 7) on 19 Nov under **guideline 4.3(b)**, saying there are already
enough dating apps. treehouse is mainly a Nigerian community app, but the listing presented it as a
dating app. This file holds the new listing and the reply to Apple. **Nothing is sent until Hydra
approves it.**

## 1. App changes (iPhone and iPad app only; Android and the website stay the same)
- **Bottom bar:** Home · Games · **Chatrooms** (raised centre) · Meet · Inbox. Before, Discover was in
  the centre.
- **Home boxes:** Chatrooms → Games → My Treehouse → Meet people → Events → treehouse Business.
  Discover is renamed "Meet people" and is no longer the first box.
- **Sign-up wording:** "Create profile" instead of "Create dating profile".
- These changes ship from the website, so the same build 7 shows them. No new upload is needed.

## 3. Reply to App Review (Resolution Center)

Hello, and thank you for the review.

We understand the concern under 4.3(b). The listing and the first screen gave the impression that treehouse is mainly a dating app. It isn’t, and we’ve corrected both.

treehouse is a community app built for Nigeria. Its main features are not found in the dating apps already on the App Store:

1. **State chatrooms:** a live public chatroom for each of Nigeria’s 36 states and the FCT, with moderation, reactions, replies, birthdays and welcomes.
2. **Real-world Treasure Hunts:** members register for hunts in their own state (registration is limited to people in that state), follow clues to physical checkpoints, collect in-app coins and compete for prizes.
3. **Multiplayer games:** ludo, chess, draughts, penalties, trivia and crosswords against other members.
4. **My Treehouse:** a community network tree, invitations and weekly community highlights.

Meeting new people is one feature among these, not the core of the app.

What we changed in this submission:
• App name and subtitle: “treehouse: Nigeria Community” / “Chatrooms, games & hunts”
• Description, keywords and screenshots now present the community features
• In the app, Chatrooms is the centre tab, and Home leads with Chatrooms, Games and My Treehouse. “Discover” is renamed “Meet people” and moved down.
• These app changes are live in build 7 now, because the interface loads from our server.

To see them, sign in with the demo account, open Chatrooms (centre tab) → Lagos, then Games → Treasure Hunt.

We’d be grateful if you could look at the app again. Thank you.
