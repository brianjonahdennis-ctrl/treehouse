Delete your account

treehouse, by Brian Dennis

Delete it yourself in the app

  1. Open treehouse (the app, or app.treehouse.ng) and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Scroll to Account and tap Delete my account.
  4. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

Or ask us to delete it

Email support@treehouse.ng from the email address you signed up with, and include your treehouse nickname. We’ll delete the account and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: email address and password.
  • Your dating profile, including your hidden first name, likes and passes.
  • Your matches, chats and message requests. Chats are deleted for both people in them.
  • Your chatroom messages and reactions.
  • Reports you made, and your blocks.

Nothing is kept after deletion, apart from daily usage totals (such as “messages sent today”), which never identified you.

Only want a break? In Profile you can hide your dating card from Discover, and turn it back on later.
