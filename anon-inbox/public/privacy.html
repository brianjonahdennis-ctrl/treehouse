Privacy policy

Last updated 11 November 2026

treehouse is an anonymous dating and chat app for adults in Nigeria. People meet under an app-given nickname and only share their first name when both people choose to reveal it. This policy explains what information treehouse handles, why, and the choices you have. treehouse is run by Brian Dennis, who you can contact at hello@treehouse.ng.

In short: other people see your nickname, never your email. Your first name is shown to a match only after you both tap “Reveal”. We don’t sell your information.

1. Information we handle

Your account

  • Your email address and password. Your password is stored by our sign-in provider in a hashed form; we can’t see it.
  • If you sign in with Google: Google tells us your email address, your name and your profile picture. We use them only to sign you in and to contact you about your account. Your name and picture are never shown to other people on treehouse, and we don’t get your Google password or anything else from your Google account.

Your dating card

  • An anonymous nickname we generate (like “Velvet Owl 342”), your age, gender, state in Nigeria, what you’re looking for, and optionally a bio and interests. Other people can see your card.
  • Your first name. It is hidden and is shown to a match only after you both choose to reveal identities.
  • Who you’d like to see (gender, age range, state), the people you like or pass on, your chats and message requests, reveal choices, blocks and reports.
  • When you were last active in the app. Other people only see whether you’re online now, or roughly how many hours ago you were active, never the exact time.
  • Your state, which you choose yourself.
  • Speed Dating (optional). If you start a three-minute video date, your camera and microphone are used only during the date, and only after you allow them. The video and sound go directly between you and the other person (through an encrypted relay when your network needs one). treehouse never records, stores or watches calls. We keep a record that the date happened (when, with which anonymous profile, and whether you each chose Like or Pass) to run matching and keep people safe, and any report you send about a date.
  • Nearby (optional, off by default). If you switch Nearby on, your phone’s location is sent once when you open Nearby and is immediately turned into a protected area of about 1 km on our server. We don’t keep your exact position. Other people only see a rounded distance and an approximate point on the map, never where you actually are. Switch Nearby off at any time and your saved area is deleted.

Chatrooms

  • Which state chatroom you’re in, the messages and reactions you post there, and reports you make. Messages are shown under your nickname to the people in that room, and are cleared after about a week.

Website and app usage

  • Daily totals such as page views and messages sent. To count unique visitors, the server keeps a scrambled code in memory for one day, made with a random value that changes daily and is never saved, so it can’t be linked back to you afterwards.
  • The treehouse owner’s personal anonymous-message page may show ads from Google AdSense. Google may use cookies or similar technology to show and measure ads. You can learn more and manage ad settings at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.
  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your device’s IP address when the page loads.
  • If you turn on push notifications, we store the address your browser gives us for sending notifications to that device. Notifications only say what happened (for example “New message”), never who it’s from or what it says. Turn them off in Profile, or by signing out on that device.

2. How we use information

  • To run treehouse: show dating cards, run chats and chatrooms, and keep you signed in.
  • To keep people safe: limit spam, review reports, and suspend links or profiles that break the rules.
  • To send account emails, such as verifying your email address or resetting your password.
  • To understand overall usage through the daily totals described above.

We don’t sell your information, and we don’t share it with advertisers.

3. Who processes it for us

  • Supabase stores account, dating and chat data and handles sign-in (data stored in Frankfurt, Germany).
  • Railway hosts the treehouse servers.
  • Cloudflare delivers treehouse’s pictures and files, and relays Speed Dating video when two phones can’t connect directly (encrypted; never recorded).
  • Resend sends account emails.
  • Google handles “Continue with Google” sign-in, if you choose it.
  • Your browser’s push service (such as Google for Chrome or Apple for Safari) delivers notifications you’ve turned on. It receives only an encrypted notice that something happened.
  • Google provides fonts, and ads on the owner’s personal message page.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect someone’s safety.

4. How long we keep it

  • Your account, dating card and chats are kept until you delete them or delete your account. Chatroom messages are cleared after about a week.
  • When you delete your account, your dating card, your matches and chats (for both people in each chat), your chatroom messages, your likes and your reports are deleted.

5. Your choices

  • Pause your dating card so you’re hidden from Discover, or leave a chatroom at any time.
  • Block, unmatch or report anyone from a chat, and block or report people in chatrooms.
  • Delete your account in the app under Profile → Delete my account. See how to delete your account.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct or delete it, by emailing support@treehouse.ng.

6. Age

treehouse is only for people 18 and over. We don’t knowingly allow anyone under 18 to create an account, and accounts we find belong to someone under 18 are removed. If you think a child is using treehouse, please email us or report them in the app.

7. Security

Connections to treehouse are encrypted, and data is protected with access rules so that people can only read their own chats and the chatroom they’re in. No service can promise perfect security, so please don’t share passwords or very sensitive personal details in messages.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top. For significant changes, we’ll let you know in the app.

9. Contact

Questions or requests: hello@treehouse.ng.
