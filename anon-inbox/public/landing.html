About treehouse

A friendlier way to meet people in Nigeria

treehouse is an anonymous social app for adults across Nigeria. Everyone gets an app-given nickname, like Velvet Owl 342, so you can be yourself without the pressure of your name, photos or followers.

Date, make friends or just talk with people in your state, your university or around the things you love. When you trust someone, you can both choose to reveal your first names. Until then, nobody knows who you are.

Safety

Anonymous, not lawless

Being anonymous should feel safe. treehouse has rules, and tools to back them up.

Read our child safety standards →

  • Adults only. You must be 18 or older to join. Accounts that appear to belong to a minor are removed.
  • Block, unmatch and report from any chat, profile or chatroom message. Reported messages are hidden for you straight away.
  • Automatic word filter in chats, chatrooms and bios.
  • Zero tolerance for harassment, scams and anything that endangers a child.
  • You control your name. It’s never shown unless you both choose to reveal.

Privacy

Your privacy, in plain English

The short version of our privacy policy. Last updated 9 November 2026.

What others see

Your nickname, age, gender, state, what you’re looking for, and your bio and interests if you add them. Never your email. Your first name only after you both reveal.

What we keep

Your email and account, your dating card, chats, likes, blocks and reports. Chatroom messages are cleared after about a week. Online status is shown roughly, never as an exact time.

Location

Only if you switch Nearby on. Your position is turned into a protected area of about 1 km straight away; we don’t keep your exact spot. Switch it off and the area is deleted.

Who helps us

Supabase stores data and handles sign-in (Frankfurt, Germany), Railway hosts our servers and Resend sends account emails. We don’t sell your information or share it with advertisers.

Notifications

Push notifications only say what happened, like “New message”, never who it’s from or what it says. Turn them off any time in Profile.

Your choices

Pause your card, leave rooms, block anyone, ask for a copy of your data, or delete your account, which removes your card, chats, messages and likes.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is treehouse free?

Yes. Creating an account, matching, chatting and joining chatrooms are all free.

Is it really anonymous?

Yes. Other people see an app-given nickname, never your email. Your first name is only shown to someone after you both tap Reveal.

Who can join?

Adults aged 18 and over living in Nigeria. You choose your state when you sign up, and that decides which state and university chatrooms you can join.

Can I use it just to make friends?

Absolutely. Add people from chatrooms as friends, share a status and chat. Dating is optional.

What if someone makes me uncomfortable?

Block or report them from the chat, their profile or any chatroom message. They aren’t told you blocked them.

Is the Android download safe?

It’s the same app we’ve sent to Google Play for review, signed with our own key. Only download it from treehouse.ng. You can compare the SHA-256 code shown under “How to install”.

When will it be on the App Store and Play Store?

Soon: both are in review. Until then, Android users can download the app directly or join the Google Play test, and everyone can use treehouse in their browser.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile → Delete my account. See how to delete your account for details.
