Child safety standards

Last updated 11 November 2026

treehouse is an anonymous dating and chat app for adults (18+) in Nigeria, run by Brian Dennis. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM). These standards explain how we keep minors off treehouse and how we act on any concern.

In short: treehouse is for adults only. Anything that sexualises, exploits or endangers a child is banned, removed immediately, and reported to the authorities. If you see something, report it in the app or email support@treehouse.ng.

1. Adults only

  • Everyone must confirm they are 18 or older to create an account, and again to create a dating card, which requires an age of 18 or over.
  • treehouse is rated 18+ on the App Store and Google Play.
  • Accounts that appear to belong to someone under 18 are suspended and removed.

2. What is prohibited

  • Any content that sexualises minors, including images, text, drawings or links, whether real or fictional.
  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) of any kind.
  • Grooming: contacting, befriending or manipulating a minor for sexual purposes, or asking anyone for content involving minors.
  • Sextortion, trafficking, or arranging to meet a minor.
  • Sharing or requesting personal information about a minor.

Breaking these rules leads to immediate, permanent removal from treehouse.

3. How to report

  • In the app: tap any chatroom message, or open the ⋯ menu in a chat, request or profile, choose Report, and pick “Seems under 18” or “Sexual or inappropriate”. Reported messages are hidden for you straight away.
  • By email: support@treehouse.ng, our designated child safety contact.

4. How we act

  • Child safety reports are reviewed first, and always within 24 hours.
  • Offending content is removed and the account is suspended and banned.
  • Objectionable words are filtered automatically in chats, chatrooms and dating bios.
  • Where we find or are told about CSAM or a child at risk, we preserve the relevant information and report it to the Nigeria Police Force and the National Agency for the Prohibition of Trafficking in Persons (NAPTIP), and to other relevant authorities as the law requires.
  • We cooperate with law enforcement requests made through proper legal channels.

5. Compliance

treehouse complies with applicable child safety laws, including Nigeria's Child Rights Act and Cybercrimes Act, and with Google Play's and Apple's child safety policies.

6. Contact

Child safety point of contact: Brian Dennis, support@treehouse.ng. See also our Terms of Use and Privacy policy.
